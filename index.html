FieldOps AI — Smart Rep Planning Dashboard

An intelligent field-force optimization platform that transforms operational data into actionable territory recommendations.

next.js tailwind shadcn/ui recharts postgresql tanstack-table hackathon-2025

FieldOps AI is a decision-support system for field representatives and territory managers. It ingests four operational datasets, computes a real-time priority score for every retailer, and surfaces the most important actions each morning — so no rep walks into the field without a plan. No heavy ML required; just smart, transparent, interpretable scoring.

Project Overview ¶

Field sales teams lose time visiting the wrong retailers at the wrong time. FieldOps AI eliminates that by processing four operational datasets offline, computing a weighted priority score for every retailer, and presenting ranked, route-optimised visit plans to each rep — and a full territory view to each manager.

User Roles ¶

The platform serves two distinct user personas, each with a dedicated dashboard.

Manager Manager

Operates at the territory level; needs a helicopter view of all reps and all retailers.

  • Territory overview with heatmap
  • Critical stock alerts across all retailers
  • Rep activity & coverage reports
  • Priority retailer list ranked by composite score

Field Rep Field Rep

Operates on the ground; needs a daily action list, not raw data.

  • Today’s optimised visit plan (ordered list)
  • Per-retailer detail: inventory, sales trend, last visit
  • Recommended actions per stop
  • Route order suggestion

Core Application Flow ¶

The system is structured as a four-step pipeline. Steps 1 and 2 run offline (or on a scheduled cron job); Steps 3 and 4 are the live UI.

Input business data (CSV datasets)
  → STEP 1: Data Processing   — compute trends, risk, inactivity, engagement
  → STEP 2: Priority Engine   — score & rank every retailer
  → STEP 3: Dashboard         — surface insights to Manager
  → STEP 4: Rep Plan          — deliver ordered visit route to Rep

Step 1 — Data Processing

Datasets are processed offline (or via a cron job). For each retailer the pipeline produces four computed signals:

SignalSource datasetFormula / logic
stock_riskretailer_inventory_weekly.csvcurrent_stock / avg_daily_sales → days to stockout
sales_velocityretailer_pos.csvweekly & monthly sales rate; trend direction
days_since_visitretailer_visit_log.csvtoday − last_visit_date
engagement_scoregrowers.csvfarm size + digital activity + high-value flag

Results are persisted to PostgreSQL and consumed by the Priority Engine.

Step 2 — Priority Engine

See the Priority Engine section for the full scoring formula.

Step 3 — Manager Dashboard

The manager sees a live view: ranked retailers, territory heatmap, low-stock alerts, rep coverage, and visit efficiency metrics. Details at Manager Dashboard →

Step 4 — Rep Visit Plan

Each rep sees their personalised daily route, sorted by priority score descending. Details at Rep Dashboard →

Datasets ¶

V1 uses exactly four datasets. That is sufficient to power every dashboard feature described in this document.

Tip Two additional datasets (whatsapp_message_log.csv and digital_funnel_weekly.csv) are reserved for the V2 AI expansion phase. Do not integrate them in V1.

1. retailer_inventory_weekly.csv

Weekly snapshot of stock levels per retailer per product SKU. Primary source for stock alerts and stockout prediction.

Computed fields

days_to_stockout = current_stock / avg_daily_sales

Example insights generated

"Retailer R22 will run out of stock in 5 days."
"Retailer R07 has zero buffer — immediate replenishment required."

Feeds into

  • Critical stock alert cards on Manager Dashboard
  • stock_risk component of priority score (weight: 40 %)
  • Retailer detail page — inventory section

2. retailer_pos.csv

Point-of-sale transaction data. Contains SKU-level sales per retailer per period. Used to assess retailer importance and product demand trends.

Computed fields

weekly_sales   = SUM(units_sold) over last 7 days
monthly_sales  = SUM(units_sold) over last 30 days
sales_velocity = weekly_sales / avg(weekly_sales across all retailers)

Example insights generated

"Retailer R41 has unusually high fungicide demand — possible emerging outbreak event."
"Retailer R88 — sales down 34 % vs prior month."

Feeds into

  • Sales trend charts on Manager Dashboard
  • sales_velocity component of priority score (weight: 30 %)
  • Retailer detail page — sales trend section

3. retailer_visit_log.csv

Timestamped log of all rep visits per retailer. Used to identify neglected retailers and measure rep coverage.

Computed fields

days_since_last_visit = today - MAX(visit_date) per retailer
visit_frequency       = COUNT(visits) over last 90 days

Example insights generated

"Retailer R19 hasn't been visited in 17 days."
"Zone 4B has had zero rep activity in the past 3 weeks."

Feeds into

  • Inactive Territory alerts on Manager Dashboard
  • days_since_visit component of priority score (weight: 20 %)
  • Rep activity table

4. growers.csv

Master list of growers linked to retailers. Contains farm size, region, and digital engagement signals. Used for grower segmentation and engagement scoring.

Computed fields

high_value_grower = farm_size > 50 acres AND revenue_tier == "A"
digitally_engaged = app_logins_last_30d > 3 OR whatsapp_active == true
large_farm        = farm_size > 100 acres
engagement_score  = weighted_sum(high_value, digital, large_farm)

Feeds into

  • Grower segmentation panel on Manager Dashboard
  • grower_engagement component of priority score (weight: 10 %)

Database Design ¶

FieldOps AI uses PostgreSQL as its primary store. Three tables hold all the computed data the dashboards need.

Table: retailers

CREATE TABLE retailers (
  id               SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
  name             TEXT NOT NULL,
  location         TEXT,           -- lat/lng or zone label
  priority_score   NUMERIC(5,2),  -- computed by Priority Engine
  days_since_visit INTEGER,        -- from visit_log dataset
  stock_risk       NUMERIC(5,2),  -- days_to_stockout normalised 0-100
  sales_velocity   NUMERIC(5,2)   -- relative to territory average
);

Table: growers

CREATE TABLE growers (
  id               SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
  retailer_id      INTEGER REFERENCES retailers(id),
  region           TEXT,
  farm_size        NUMERIC(8,2),  -- acres
  engagement_level TEXT           -- "high" | "medium" | "low"
);

Table: visits

CREATE TABLE visits (
  id           SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
  rep_id       INTEGER NOT NULL,
  retailer_id  INTEGER REFERENCES retailers(id),
  visit_date   DATE NOT NULL,
  notes        TEXT
);
Note Re-run the Priority Engine after every dataset refresh to keep retailers.priority_score current. In V1 this can be a nightly cron job.

Priority Engine — “The AI” ¶

The Priority Engine is the intelligence layer of FieldOps AI. It is a transparent, weighted scoring function — not a black-box model — which makes it auditable, explainable, and easy to tune without retraining.

Presentation framing Do not say “hardcoded logic.” Present it as an “intelligent prioritization engine that dynamically weights operational signals to surface the highest-impact retailer visits.”

Scoring Formula

priority_score =
  ( stock_risk × 0.40 ) +
  ( sales_velocity × 0.30 ) +
  ( days_since_visit × 0.20 ) +
  ( grower_engagement × 0.10 )

Weight rationale

SignalWeightWhy
stock_risk40 %A stockout is the highest-urgency event — it directly costs revenue and grower trust.
sales_velocity30 %High-velocity retailers need attention to prevent demand gaps; low velocity may signal relationship issues.
days_since_visit20 %Relationship decay is real — prolonged absence erodes brand loyalty and share of shelf.
grower_engagement10 %High-value, digitally-engaged growers merit proactive rep attention.

Output query

-- Sort descending — highest urgency first
SELECT id, name, priority_score
FROM   retailers
ORDER  BY priority_score DESC
LIMIT  10;

The score is written back to retailers.priority_score after each engine run and consumed directly by both dashboards.

Getting Started ¶

Prerequisites

  • Node.js ≥ 18
  • PostgreSQL 16 (local install or Docker)
  • The four V1 CSV datasets placed in /data/

Installation

# 1. Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/your-org/fieldops-ai.git
cd fieldops-ai

# 2. Install dependencies
npm install

# 3. Configure environment variables
cp .env.example .env.local
# Open .env.local and fill in DATABASE_URL and NEXTAUTH_SECRET

# 4. Apply the database schema
npm run db:migrate

# 5. Process CSV datasets and seed the database
npm run data:process

# 6. Start the development server
npm run dev
# → http://localhost:3000
Note npm run data:process reads the four CSV files from /data/, computes all derived signals (stock_risk, sales_velocity, days_since_visit, engagement_score), runs the Priority Engine, and writes results to PostgreSQL. Re-run this command whenever the CSVs are refreshed.

Environment variables

VariableRequiredDescription
DATABASE_URLYesPostgreSQL connection string
NEXTAUTH_SECRETYesSession signing secret (min 32 chars)
NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URLNoBase URL for OAuth callbacks
DATA_DIRNoOverride default /data/ path for CSVs

Future Expansion — V2 AI Features ¶

These features are out of scope for V1 but explicitly planned. Mention them during your presentation to demonstrate forward-thinking product design.

  • WhatsApp Sentiment Analysis — whatsapp_message_log.csv fed into an NLP classifier to detect retailer dissatisfaction signals early
  • Digital Funnel Intelligence — digital_funnel_weekly.csv used to predict grower conversion likelihood and prioritise digital outreach
  • Route Optimisation — Leaflet + TSP algorithm to minimise travel distance across the day’s visit list
  • Anomaly Detection — automatically flag statistically unusual sales spikes (e.g. R41 fungicide demand surge)
  • Rep Performance Prediction — forecast territory score improvement based on a proposed visit plan

How to Present FieldOps AI ¶

The framing matters as much as the code. Follow these guidelines during your demo and pitch.

Do NOT say “We hardcoded some logic to sort retailers.”
DO say “FieldOps AI is an intelligent field-force optimization platform that transforms operational data into actionable territory recommendations — enabling reps to always visit the right retailer, at the right time, for the right reason.”

Key talking points

  • The Priority Engine dynamically weights four operational signals — stock risk, sales velocity, recency of visit, and grower engagement — into a single, explainable score
  • The system processes real agrochemical datasets and surfaces insights that would otherwise require hours of manual spreadsheet analysis
  • The architecture is extensible — V2 will layer NLP-based sentiment from WhatsApp data and digital funnel intelligence on top of the same scoring framework
  • The UI is designed to feel enterprise-grade: clean, data-driven, and decision-focused

Suggested demo order

  1. Land on the Manager Dashboard — highlight the critical alert cards and the priority-sorted table
  2. Click a red retailer row → open Retailer Detail page and walk through the score breakdown radar chart
  3. Switch to the Rep view — show today’s ordered visit plan and recommended actions
  4. Show the Territory Heatmap — red / amber / green zones at a glance
  5. Close with the V2 roadmap: WhatsApp NLP, route optimisation, anomaly detection
